Game Genres Explained

A genre is a group of games that play in a similar way. Here are the most popular ones.

Platformer

JumpRun

You run and jump across platforms, dodge hazards and reach the end of each level. Timing is everything.

Role-Playing Game (RPG)

StoryLevel up

You play a character, make choices, gain experience and grow stronger while exploring a big world.

Shooter

ActionAim

Fast reflexes and accurate aim matter most, either alone against computer enemies or with teams online.

Puzzle

ThinkRelax

Solve problems with logic, patterns or clever tricks. No rush, just the satisfying click of figuring it out.

Strategy

PlanBuild

Manage resources, build bases or armies and outsmart opponents by planning several steps ahead.

Racing

SpeedSkill

Learn the track, master the corners and beat the clock or other drivers to the finish line.

Which one should you try?

  • Like stories? Try an RPG.
  • Like quick, short rounds? Try a platformer or racing game.
  • Like thinking slowly? Try puzzle or strategy.
