Gaming Tips

Playing is more fun when you feel good. These habits help you enjoy games for longer.

Healthy habits

  • Take a short break every hour: stand up, stretch and look away from the screen.
  • Drink water and keep a snack nearby that isn't too sugary.
  • Sit with your back supported and keep the screen at eye level.
  • Finish your daily tasks first, then play guilt-free.
  • Be kind online: mute rude players and don't share personal details.

Getting better at games

  • Practice the basics before trying the hardest mode.
  • Watch how stronger players solve a tricky part, then try your own way.
  • Don't give up after losing. Every restart teaches something.
