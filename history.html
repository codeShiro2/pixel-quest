A Short History of Video Games

Video games have changed a lot in just a few decades. Here are some big moments, in order.

  1. 1950s-60s: Scientists build simple experimental games on huge university computers.
  2. 1970s: Arcade machines appear in shops and cafes, and home consoles start to arrive.
  3. 1980s: Home gaming grows fast, and games become a regular family hobby.
  4. 1990s: 3D graphics arrive, and discs and handheld systems become popular.
  5. 2000s: Online play, motion controls and phone games bring in new players.
  6. 2010s-today: Digital stores, streaming and indie developers reshape how games are made and shared.
