About Pixel Quest

Pixel Quest is a fan site for anyone curious about video games. It explains the basics in plain language so newcomers can find their way and longtime players can look back at how far games have come.

Games are more than a way to pass time. They tell stories, test reflexes and creativity, and let friends who live far apart play together.

What you will find here

  • A guide to the main game genres.
  • A timeline of gaming history, from arcades to today.
  • A table of famous game consoles.
  • A list of top-rated games.
  • Healthy gaming tips and a sample weekly schedule.
