Gaming Glossary

New to gaming? Here are common terms you will hear, explained simply.

Common video game terms
Term Meaning
NPCNon-player character: a character controlled by the game, not by a person.
HPHealth points: how much damage a character can take before losing.
XPExperience points: earned by playing, used to level up.
BossA strong enemy, usually found at the end of a level.
RespawnComing back into the game after losing a life.
Co-opCooperative play: players work together as a team.
IndieA game made by a small, independent team.
SpeedrunFinishing a game as fast as possible.
